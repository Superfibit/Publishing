## A) 14-Day Launch/Sprint Plan

### Days 1–2: Conversion Foundation
1. **Product page tune-up**
   - Replace opening blurb lines with a stronger high-stakes hook.
   - Ensure genre markers appear naturally: “epic science fantasy,” “portal fantasy,” “ancient relics.”
2. **Metadata sanity check**
   - Confirm subtitle clarity and consistency across ebook/paperback.
   - Validate backend keyword coverage without stuffing.
3. **Author Central setup**
   - Publish bio (use one of the versions below).
   - Add profile image + 1–3 book-relevant visuals.

### Days 3–4: Ad Launch
1. **Campaign 1 — Auto Discovery**
   - Budget: $6/day
   - Goal: mine search term data
2. **Campaign 2 — Manual Broad/Phrase**
   - Budget: $8/day
   - Seed keywords from target clusters
3. **Campaign 3 — Manual Exact (High intent)**
   - Budget: $6/day
   - Tight list of strongest genre/comparable intent terms

**Total ad cap:** $20/day max

### Days 5–7: Observe, Don’t Overreact
- No bid changes before meaningful data accrues.
- Add negatives only for clearly irrelevant terms.
- Maintain stable pricing during this first read window.

### Day 8 Checkpoint (First Optimization)
- Pause keywords with high spend + zero orders.
- Increase bids modestly (+10–15%) on terms with best CTR and conversions.
- Split top 5 search terms into exact match if they came from auto/broad.

### Days 9–12: Organic Assist Layer
- Publish 2 short creator posts:
  1) “Why I wrote Neviath”
  2) “If you like X/Y/Z, here’s what Neviath delivers”
- Use soft CTA only (no spam, no pressure).

### Day 14 Checkpoint (Decision Gate)
Declare **Win / Iterate / Rebuild** based on:
- CTR >= 0.30% (workable)
- At least 1 conversion path showing traction
- ACOS trend improving vs day 3–4 baseline
- Reviews and KU pages moving upward

If above threshold: scale winners by 15–25% budget.
If below: rework hook/copy + tighten targeting.

### 250 words
Matthew Lancaster writes emotionally charged epic science fantasy centered on identity, survival, and the search for belonging. His debut novel, *Neviath*, is Book One in a planned seven-book saga that combines portal fantasy, relic-based science-fantasy systems, and character-first storytelling.

In *Neviath*, a foster teen is pulled from Earth into a dying alien world sustained by ancient structures no one fully understands anymore. When those relics begin to fail, she discovers they respond to her in ways no one expected—making her both a target and a final hope.

Lancaster’s fiction explores a core question: what happens when someone who has always felt disposable is suddenly asked to carry the weight of a world? Rather than relying on simple destiny tropes, his stories emphasize earned strength, emotional consequence, and the cost of power.

His work is aimed at readers who love epic scale with intimate stakes—fans of science-fantasy worlds, dangerous mysteries, and coming-of-age arcs that refuse easy answers. Outside of writing, he researches worldbuilding frameworks, narrative systems, and emerging creative tools that expand how stories are imagined and shared.

## D) Positioning Brief

## Market Position
**Category:** Epic Science Fantasy (Portal Fantasy + Relic Quest + Coming-of-Age)

## Strategic Comparables
- N.K. Jemisin (*Broken Earth*) — world instability + emotional gravity
- Pierce Brown (*Red Rising*) — youth under pressure + brutal escalation
- Brandon Sanderson (Mistborn-era appeal) — system intrigue + layered power mechanics

## Target Reader
- Ages 16–35
- Readers of epic fantasy/sci-fantasy who want emotional depth, not only action
- Fans of survival arcs, power awakening, and high-stakes world collapse narratives

## Core Promise
A teen who felt unwanted on Earth becomes indispensable in a world on the edge of extinction.

## Emotional Hook
“Being chosen” is not the fantasy here.
**Becoming necessary after a lifetime of abandonment is.**

## Content Warnings (disclose transparently)
- Fantasy violence
- Emotional trauma/abandonment themes
- Psychological manipulation
- Brief blood/injury
